About

Models of gene regulation have become strikingly good at prediction, yet the distance between a model that predicts well and one that explains how regulation actually works remains wide.

The aim is to discuss the cutting-edge problems standing between current models and a genuine understanding of genome regulation: what our models have learned, what they demonstrably have not, and which open questions are worth attacking next.
